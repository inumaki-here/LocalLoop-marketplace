# LocalLoop-marketplace
A web-based marketplace connecting local vendors with nearby customers.
The Local Market Online Store for Small Vendors is a web-based marketplace designed to help small businesses and local vendors establish a digital presence and reach more customers. Vendors can create store profiles, add and manage products, maintain inventory, receive orders, and promote their products online. Customers can browse products from local vendors, compare prices, add items to a shopping cart, place orders, make secure digital payments, track deliveries, and provide feedback. The system includes vendor registration, store and product catalog management, inventory tracking, shopping cart, payment integration, order management, promotions, customer reviews, notifications, and an admin dashboard. The primary objective is to increase the digital reach of local businesses and provide customers with a convenient platform for discovering and purchasing products from nearby vendors.
